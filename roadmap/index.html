Every job between your idea and your release

Nine stages, in the order most independent films go through them, and the jobs inside each one. Where a rule decides what you can do, it is quoted word for word from the official source. Tick what you have done: your ticks stay on this device only.

Sources checked 24 October 20260 of 0 done

    1Idea and script

    Done when you have a script you would show a financier, a logline and a one-page synopsis.

    • “The short film world has almost no financing infrastructure behind it. Features do.”No Film School, 13 April 2026
    • “Telefilm will not accept projects where AI substantially dominates or replaces the creative process to the extent that the project is primarily created by AI.”Telefilm Canada, AI policy, November 2025
      Italy’s 2026 selective grants do not accept development projects “i cui materiali artistici sono realizzati mediante l’uso di sistemi di intelligenza artificiale” (whose artistic materials are made with AI systems).MiC, DGCA, 2026 call (decree of 15 May 2026)

    2Rights and company

    Done when you can show, on paper, that you control every right in the story, and there is a company able to sign contracts and apply for money.

    • “You will need to send us a chain of title summary that outlines the flow of ownership in the underlying rights in and to the project.”BFI Discovery feature funding, application page
    • The company must “own 100% of the rights of the project (unless the project is an audiovisual treaty coproduction)”.Telefilm Canada, Talent to Watch 2026-2027 guidelines
      “Director/producers are not eligible to apply.”BFI NETWORK England short film funding

    3Budget and schedule

    Done when you have a scene by scene breakdown, a shooting schedule and a budget that runs to the release, not just to the last day of shooting.

    • “A great camera is a fun idea, but you may want your money to go further into other items, like overtime, extra shoot days, and food.”No Film School, 28 February 2025
    • “We vastly underestimated how big of a mountain post is when you have no money.” They had money left for an editor or a composer, not both.No Film School, Midnight Taxi, 2 August 2024
    • They “are increasingly standard deliverables to reach wide audiences and are now required by many film festivals, awards bodies, television networks, and streaming platforms.”International Documentary Association, 19 December 2024
    • Hundreds of Beavers (2024) cost $150,000 to make; its self-release cost “approximately $135,000”.Stephen Follows, 15 July 2026

    4Financing

    Done when the money for the shoot is confirmed in writing, not just promised.

    • “Having the first money in is the hardest thing for me to get in my day-to-day.” For a film starring Channing Tatum and Gemma Chan, he still went to “30 or 40 investors”.No Film School, interview with WME Independent agent Abe Bengio, 13 April 2026
    • “Indiegogo charges 5% of the collected amount”, plus 3% of each payment and 0.20 per transaction for processing.Indiegogo help centre, updated 13 October 2026
      Produzioni dal Basso keeps 5% in its reward and all-or-nothing modes and 3% for simple donations, plus payment costs.Produzioni dal Basso, how it works
    • “A minimum of 30% of the financing, or at least the local production support from your national film fund, must already be secured.”Berlinale Co-Production Market, 2026 FAQ
    • The micro-budget comedy Sour Party was financed with “a small-business loan, credit cards, deferred rates, and community support”.No Film School, 13 July 2026

    5Pre-production

    Done when cast, crew and locations are confirmed, contracts are signed and the production is insured.

    • “The best way to assess your needs is to consult an experienced insurance broker who specializes in media or entertainment about the risks related to your project.”International Documentary Association, 19 December 2024
    • On Musicbed, for example, “Cinema / Film Festival” use is only in the Enterprise plan, priced on request.Musicbed pricing page

    6Shooting

    Done when everything is shot, every file exists in at least two places and every release form is filed.

    7Post-production

    Done when you have a locked cut, a final mix, a graded master, cleared music and the files festivals and buyers ask for.

    • “Applying for E&O insurance can take weeks and often requires an opinion letter from an experienced entertainment clearance attorney.”International Documentary Association, 19 December 2024

    8Festivals

    Done when you have a ranked festival list, a premiere plan and a budget for the fees.

    • Of 10,549 festivals studied, “only 11.1% actively say they do not accept films that are already online”, but “Cannes, Berlin, Venice, and Sundance all reject feature films that have been publicly available online before their premiere.”Stephen Follows, 20 November 2025
    • “The average submission fees for short films are between $30 and $55. For features it’s between $47 and $87.” (4,631 festivals)Stephen Follows, 22 September 2025
    • The submission platform Festhome reports “2200 fraudulent festivals detected and rejected” by its staff.Festhome home page

    9Sales and release

    Done when the film can be watched by the audience you made it for, through a distributor or on your own.

    • Of the fiction features made in 2024, 8.9% have a professional sales agent so far, a figure that “will still tick up a little”.Stephen Follows, 31 August 2026
    • “Beware of sales agents that charge an upfront fee. That is not standard.”Distribution Advocates, 31 January 2024
    • A distribution agreement “does not necessarily include a minimum guarantee or a marketing budget”.No Film School, Sour Party, 13 July 2026
    • “You need to seriously budget for marketing.” The films that did best “had already raised money before they needed it.” (57 case studies)Stephen Follows, 15 July 2026
